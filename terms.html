Concord Plus

Concord Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription that opens the comparison layer of the results screens: how everyone answered a question or bill, how people who lean left, mixed, and right split on it, and how states compare. Everything about your own views, answers, and Compass stays free with or without Plus. Comparisons appear only once enough people have answered a question, so Plus opens more over time as the sample grows; it does not create data that is not there.

Plus is sold through Apple’s in-app purchase system and billed to your Apple ID at the price shown in the app at the time of purchase. Payment is charged when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your device Settings under your Apple ID and Subscriptions; cancelling stops future renewals and Plus stays open until the end of the period you paid for.

One comparison may be shown free, once, so you can see what Plus opens before deciding. Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple’s policies. Plus is available on the platforms and in the countries where Apple offers it.
